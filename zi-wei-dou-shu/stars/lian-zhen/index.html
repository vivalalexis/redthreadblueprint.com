Lian Zhen 廉贞

Reformer. Lian Zhen 廉贞 governs standards, transformation, discipline, allure, and the tendency that comes with it is rigidity or rebellion, all-or-nothing intensity.

What Lian Zhen is

Lian Zhen is the most double-edged of the fourteen, and the texts read it two ways depending on company: as principle, or as the appetite that principle is holding down. Its palace is where you have strong views and strong pull.

Both readings share a mechanism, which is intensity that does not moderate itself. It arrives as discipline or as rebellion and rarely as anything in between. What a reading looks for is which of the two the surrounding stars are feeding.

How it reads in a chart

A star on its own is a tendency with no subject. What gives it one is the palace it lands in, which of the twelve areas of a life that palace covers, and how brightly the star sits there, since the same star at its strongest and at its weakest are not describing the same person. A reading states the brightness before it states the meaning.

Common questions

Notes
Is Lian Zhen a good star to have?

No star in this system is good or bad on its own. Each one is a set of tendencies with a matching cost, and what decides the reading is the palace it lands in, how brightly it sits there, and what else is in the palace with it.

What does Lian Zhen mean in my career palace, or my wealth palace?

The star supplies the tendency and the palace supplies the subject, so the same star reads differently in each of the twelve. That combination is what a full Zi Wei Dou Shu reading walks through, one palace at a time.
