The Year Ahead

A yearly forecast usually tells you what will happen. This one tells you when the year is with you and when it's against you, which is the part you can use.

Your chart meets a specific year, and the interaction is particular to you rather than to everyone born under the same animal. This reads the year's pillar against your own, then lays the twelve months out as a drawn timeline: the stretches that favour pushing, the stretches that favour patience, and the one or two things that genuinely change for you.

Common questions

Notes
Which year does it cover, and when does it start?

The solar year, which turns at Li Chun in early February, not on 1 January. The document says which dates it runs between on its cover, and the months are laid out inside on that same basis.

Is this the same as a zodiac-sign forecast for my animal?

No. A sign forecast is one of twelve, written for everyone born in a twelve-month span. This is computed against your own four pillars, which is why one of its sections is called your year, not your sign's year.

What if I do not know my birth time?

Write unknown. The hour pillar is withheld and the reading says plainly which parts it can and cannot speak to. Nothing is invented to fill the gap.

How is this different from a free chatbot reading?

Ask a chatbot for your chart and it invents one, then invents a different one tomorrow. The chart here is calculated by the same deterministic library every time, and the working is printed in the back so you can check it.

Does a caesarean or induced birth change the chart?

No. The chart is cast from the time you were born, however that happened.
