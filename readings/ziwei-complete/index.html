Zi Wei Dou Shu Reading 紫微斗數

紫微斗數 zi wei dou shu: the purple star astrolabe, a life sorted into twelve palaces.

Some people find element-counting abstract. They want their life addressed by area: this part is work, this part is money, this part is the people close to them. Zi Wei Dou Shu is built that way. Twelve palaces, one per area of a life, each with its own stars sitting in it.

This reading walks all twelve and tells you what's in each, in plain English. Every star name appears in English beside its character. Where a palace is strong you're told what that tends to buy you. Where it's crowded or empty, you're told that too.

Common questions

Notes
How is this different from a BaZi or Saju reading?

It sorts a life by area rather than by element. Zi Wei Dou Shu places twelve palaces, one for career, one for wealth, one for the people closest to you, and reads the stars sitting in each. People who find element counting abstract usually find this easier to use.

Will the star names mean anything to me?

Each star is named in Chinese with its English reading beside it, and described by what it does in the palace it occupies rather than by its reputation. One section exists specifically to leave you with star names you can use.

What if I do not know my birth time?

This is the one reading that cannot be produced without it. The twelve palaces are placed from the hour you were born, so with no hour there is no astrolabe to read and nothing honest to write. A BaZi or Saju reading works from the date alone and is the right choice if the time is genuinely unrecoverable.

How is this different from a free chatbot reading?

Ask a chatbot for your chart and it invents one, then invents a different one tomorrow. The chart here is calculated by the same deterministic library every time, and the working is printed in the back so you can check it.

Does a caesarean or induced birth change the chart?

No. The chart is cast from the time you were born, however that happened.
