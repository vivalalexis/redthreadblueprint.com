Eight Mansions for Two 八宅

八宅 ba zhai: the eight mansions, favourable and unfavourable directions from a birth year.

Two people, one home, and every direction favours exactly one of you. That's more common than people expect, and it's the case this reading is built for.

Free Kua calculators give each of you a number and no way to read them together. This puts both compasses on one map: whose wall the bed goes against, which desk faces where, how the table seats you both well, and what to check at a viewing when you're looking as a pair.

Nothing in it ranks you against each other. Verdicts describe walls, never people. Where a direction suits one of you and not the other, the document says so, says what that costs, and gives you a way to split it: assign by use, fix by facing, and let your preferences outvote the compass.

Common questions

Notes
Will this tell me how the other person feels about me?

No. It reads two birth charts against each other and describes how the two patterns behave in the same room. It is not a way to find out what someone privately thinks, and it will not be written that way.

Does it work for same-sex couples?

Yes. The calculation basis sets the direction of the traditional cycle only, it is chosen per person, and the written reading is gender neutral throughout.

What if we are both in the same group?

Then the same directions favour both of you and the document says so early, because it changes the whole job. The harder and more common case is a split pairing, where every direction favours exactly one of you, which is what the section on splitting what cannot be shared is for.

What if I do not know my birth time?

Write unknown. The hour pillar is withheld and the reading says plainly which parts it can and cannot speak to. Nothing is invented to fill the gap.

How is this different from a free chatbot reading?

Ask a chatbot for your chart and it invents one, then invents a different one tomorrow. The chart here is calculated by the same deterministic library every time, and the working is printed in the back so you can check it.

Does a caesarean or induced birth change the chart?

No. The chart is cast from the time you were born, however that happened.
