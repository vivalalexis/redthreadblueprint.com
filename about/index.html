About

Charts computed properly, then written so you can use them.

Red Thread Blueprint turns a birth moment into a document. The chart is calculated by an engine that gives the same answer every time. What it means is written against classical source texts, set as a drawing set, and delivered as a PDF you keep.

The problem it was built for

01

These systems are old, precise, and largely locked behind two doors: a language you may not read, and a practitioner you may not be able to reach. The usual English options are a free calculator that hands you eight characters and stops, or a chatbot that will produce a confident chart and a different confident chart an hour later.

Neither gives you the thing that is actually hard, which is putting the chart into words. That is the whole job here.

No travel, no appointment, no Korean

02

There is no waiting list, no consultation to book, and no requirement that you read Korean or Chinese. Every character that appears in your document is translated where it sits. You send four pieces of information and a designed PDF comes back within twenty four hours.

Two halves, kept apart

03

The calculation and the interpretation are separate on purpose. Charts are computed by a deterministic astronomical library, corrected for true solar time at your birth longitude, and verified against a fixed set of known charts before any release. The reading is then written against the classical texts named in your document, and reviewed against your own chart before the file is issued. The free tools on this site run the same computation as the paid readings.

How the documents are produced is set out in full on the method page, down to the source texts each section is written against.

What it will not do

04

It does not predict, it does not instruct, and it does not flatter. Readings are prepared for subjects aged eighteen and over. Where a chart carries something difficult, the document says so plainly and pairs it with something you can act on, because a reading that only tells you what you want to hear is worth nothing.

Where it lives

05

Readings are sold through the shop, which handles checkout, delivery and buyer protection. Questions go to tk@redthreadblueprint.com. The free tools and the reference library stay here, free to use, with no account and nothing stored.
