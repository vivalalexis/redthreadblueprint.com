The ten gods

A BaZi chart has eight characters and one of them is you. The ten gods are the ten possible relations between that character and every other one, and they are the vocabulary the whole reading runs on. Each is a tendency, never a verdict.

Where the ten come from

Two questions produce all of them. Is the other stem's element the same as your day master's, or one you generate, control, are generated by, or are controlled by? And does its polarity match yours or oppose it?

Five relations times two polarities is ten, and that is the entire set. There is nothing else in it. This is why the vocabulary is worth learning once: every statement a BaZi reading makes about what a chart contains is made in these ten words.

Why a name is not a verdict

The English names circulating for these are unhelpful, and some are actively misleading. Reading them as good or bad is the single most common error people make with this system, and the classical texts are explicit that it is wrong.

The same god reads in opposite directions depending on whether the chart can carry it. Pressure in a chart with the support to hold it is resilience; in one without, it is strain. Support in a stretched chart is relief; in an already comfortable one it is stagnation. The relation does not change. What changes is the balance around it, and working that out is what a reading is for.
