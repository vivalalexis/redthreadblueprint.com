The eight directions

Eight Mansions gives every person one number, and that number turns the compass into eight assignments: four directions that work for you and four that do not. The compass belongs to the person, never to the building, which is why the same wall reads two opposite ways.

The eight wandering stars

Computed · 遊星

Each direction carries one of these eight for you, decided by your Kua number. Four are favourable and four are rough, and the tradition ranks them rather than sorting them into two piles. The classical name sits beside each, because the literal translations are dramatic and the applied reading is what a chart actually supports.

The two life groups

Computed · Exact complements

Every Kua number belongs to one of two groups, and the two sets of four directions do not overlap anywhere. That is why a household with one person in each group has no shared favourable ground, and why the work becomes assigning rooms rather than finding a compromise wall.

The compass belongs to the person

This is the thing most direction writing gets backwards. Eight Mansions as practised here reads the compass out from the individual, not from the building. There is no such thing as a good north-facing flat, only a flat whose north wall happens to suit the person standing at it.

A separate school reads the dwelling's own trigram instead. That is a different tradition with a different question, and mixing the two produces contradictions rather than a fuller picture.

Assignment, not avoidance

Every home contains all eight directions. You cannot subtract four of them, and no honest reading asks you to try. The four that do not favour you are ranked by severity precisely because most homes force a compromise somewhere, and it is better to make the cheap one on purpose.

What the tradition actually offers is assignment: which room is used for what, which wall a desk faces, which one a headboard sits against. The scale of an answer is a chair turned or a bed rotated. It is never a renovation, and it is never a directive about how to live.
