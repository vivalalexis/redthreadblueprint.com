Earth 土

tǔ

The element that holds. Earth takes from Fire, gives to Metal, contains Water, and is the only element carried by four of the twelve branches.

What earth is

Earth takes from Fire and gives to Metal in the feeding cycle 生. In the checking cycle 剋 it contains Water, and Wood works on it.

The word that fits it is holding. Earth is where things stay long enough to become something: the element that does not convert what it receives so much as keep it. That is why it reads as reliability, and also why it is the element most easily mistaken for inertia.

One plain fact sets it apart. Four of the twelve earthly branches carry earth, where every other element has two. Earth is simply more present in the raw material of charts than anything else is.

How it reads in a chart

Earth reads as steadiness, keeping, and being the thing others build on: work with continuity in it, roles where the value is in still being there. Classical practice associates it with trust and with the middle of things, in the sense of what holds a structure together rather than what leads it.

The two stems separate cleanly. Yang Earth 戊 is ground that does not move, and its strength is that it does not. Yin Earth 己 is ground that is worked: it takes an impression, holds what is put in it, and gives something back.

Why the season changes the answer

Earth in an earth season is at its peak. In a fire season it is supported, because fire feeds it. In a wood season it is exhausted, because wood works directly on it, and in a metal season it is resting, giving to metal without being taken apart.

That spread matters more for earth than for most, because earth is so often present. Quantity is not strength. A chart can be full of earth and still have all of it in the wrong season, which is precisely the case a summary of your element will never catch.

Too much earth, and too little

Too much earth with nothing to move it reads as holding past the point of use: arrangements kept because they were already there. What that chart is short of is usually wood, which is the element that breaks ground and gives earth something to hold that is worth holding.

Too little earth is not unreliability. It more often reads as a chart with no settled ground of its own, where things are converted or spent rather than kept, and where the structure has to come from outside.

Neither is a verdict, and which one describes you is decided by the season and by the seven characters around your day master. That is the reading, and it is not something this page can do for you.

Common questions

Notes
Why does earth have four animal signs when the others have two?

Because the twelve earthly branches do not divide evenly by five. The Ox 丑, Dragon 辰, Goat 未 and Dog 戌 all carry earth as their principal element, which means earth turns up in a chart more often than any other element does. This page reads the five in a single cycle, so it takes that as the plain arithmetic it is rather than as a claim about earth's rank.

Does an earth element mean I am stubborn?

No. Earth describes holding rather than resisting, and those are different things. A chart heavy in earth tends to be the one others rely on to stay put, which is only stubbornness when the situation called for movement. Whether it did is a question about the chart and the timing, not about the element.

Which animal signs are earth?

The Ox 丑, the Dragon 辰, the Goat 未 and the Dog 戌. Those four earthly branches carry earth as their principal element, which is a separate question from the year you were born in.

Earth through the five seasons

Computed · 旺相休囚死

The month you were born in commands an element, and that decides how much your own has to work with. Classical practice grades it in five states, and it is the first thing a reading works out. Read down for the month, across for what it does to earth.
