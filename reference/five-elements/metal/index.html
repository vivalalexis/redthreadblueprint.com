Metal 金

jīn

The element that decides. Metal takes from Earth, gives to Water, cuts Wood back, and is the one element Fire reshapes.

What metal is

Metal takes from Earth and gives to Water in the feeding cycle 生. In the checking cycle 剋 it cuts Wood back, and Fire reshapes it.

The idea underneath it is separation. Metal is the element that makes a distinction and then holds it: this counts, that does not. Everything it is associated with, from precision to standards to the ability to end something, comes out of that one function.

Its relationship with fire is worth naming. Fire is the only thing that changes metal’s form, and it does not destroy it. A chart with both is a chart where judgement is repeatedly tested and repeatedly remade.

How it reads in a chart

Metal reads as judgement, precision and the willingness to draw a line: finishing, editing, deciding what is good enough. Classical practice associates it with justice and with the harvest, which is the same idea twice, taking what is ready and leaving the rest.

The two stems are genuinely different. Yang Metal 庚 is unworked and blunt, and its strength is force rather than finish. Yin Metal 辛 is metal that has already been refined, and its strength is exactness. Neither is the more capable one.

Why the season changes the answer

Metal in a metal season is at its peak. In an earth season it is supported, because earth feeds it. In a fire season it is exhausted, and in a wood season it is held back, working on something it can reduce but not get through.

This is where the element on its own stops being useful. Metal with the season behind it is decisive. The same metal with the season against it is the person who can see exactly what should be cut and cannot make the cut. The element is identical in both. The season is not.

Too much metal, and too little

Too much metal, with nothing to soften it, tends to read as a standard applied past its usefulness: things ended early, options narrowed before they were understood. What that chart is often short of is fire, which is what reshapes metal rather than merely resisting it.

Too little metal is not a lack of discipline. It reads more often as difficulty finishing: many things begun and carried a long way, with the final decision left open because nothing in the chart forces it.

Both are ordinary and both are workable. Which one is yours depends on your season and on the seven characters sitting around your day master, and that is a reading rather than a definition.

Common questions

Notes
Is metal a cold or unfeeling element?

No, and that reading comes from the English word rather than from the practice. Metal describes discrimination, the act of separating what counts from what does not. That can look like reserve from outside, but it is a description of judgement, not of feeling. Charts heavy in metal are often the ones holding a standard nobody else is willing to hold.

Which animal signs are metal?

The Monkey 申 and the Rooster 酉 carry metal as their principal element. That is a fact about those two earthly branches. How much metal you hold depends on which branches and stems appear across all four pillars of your own chart.

Why is metal described as cutting wood?

Because in the checking cycle 剋 metal is what gives wood a limit. Wood grows in every available direction and metal decides which of those directions survives. In a chart that reads as the difference between having ideas and finishing one of them, which is why a wood chart short of metal is a recognisable pattern.

Metal through the five seasons

Computed · 旺相休囚死

The month you were born in commands an element, and that decides how much your own has to work with. Classical practice grades it in five states, and it is the first thing a reading works out. Read down for the month, across for what it does to metal.

木 In a wood month
囚 Held back

it checks the season and the season is bigger, so the effort goes nowhere

火 In a fire month
死 Exhausted

the season checks it directly, and it has the least to work with

土 In an earth month
相 Supported

the season feeds it, so it has more behind it than it brought

金 In a metal month
旺 At its peak

the season is its own element, and it is as strong as it gets

水 In a water month
休 Resting

it feeds the season, which draws on it without taking it apart

This is one input of several. Seasonal status says what metal has behind it, not whether the chart is strong, which also takes the other seven characters into account.
