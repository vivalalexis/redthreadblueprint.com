The systems

5 systems

Each system has its own page and its own reference sets, listed under it below. Every set is free to read, with each character translated where it sits.

BaZi 八字

Eight characters, four pillars, one per unit of time you were born into. BaZi reads a life through the five elements in relation to one another. Open the system →

Korean Saju 사주

Saju 사주 means four pillars. The chart is the same eight characters BaZi computes; the tradition reading them is Korean. Open the system →

Zi Wei Dou Shu 紫微斗數

Twelve palaces, one per area of a life, each with its own stars sitting in it. Zi Wei Dou Shu sorts a life by area rather than by element. Open the system →

Qi Men Dun Jia 奇門遁甲

Qi Men Dun Jia does not start from your birth. It casts a plate for the moment a question is asked, which makes it the one system here aimed at a decision rather than a person. Open the system →

Eight Mansions 八宅

Eight Mansions is its own system, not a branch of the four pillars. It works from one number and answers a different question: which directions suit you. Open the system →

Where systems differ

All 5 →

What is a day master?

Of the eight characters in a BaZi or Saju chart, one is you. The rest describe what that one is standing in. Read →

What is saju?

Saju 사주 means four pillars. It is the Korean practice of reading the eight characters of a birth moment, and in Korea it is checked before decisions rather than consulted for entertainment. Read →

Saju and BaZi, where they differ

The most common question about these two systems has a short answer that surprises people: the chart is the same. Choosing between them is choosing a tradition to read in, not a more accurate calculation. Read →

Zi Wei Dou Shu and BaZi, which answers what

These are not competing methods with the same output. They organise a life along different axes, which is why practitioners who use both do not treat one as a check on the other. Read →

BaZi and Western astrology, where they differ

They share a premise, that the moment you were born is worth recording, and almost nothing else. The differences are structural rather than a matter of style. Read →
