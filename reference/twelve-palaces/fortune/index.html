Fortune 福德

fú dé

Inner contentment, enjoyment, and what actually restores you. Not luck, despite the name it usually travels under.

What the Fortune palace covers

福德 reads inner contentment: what you enjoy, what restores you, and the quality of your own company. The English name it usually travels under does it no favours, because it is not a luck palace and reading it as one wastes the most humane part of the system.

The subject is closer to what makes a life feel worth the effort. Classical practice treats that as a readable quality rather than a mood.

Read it with Wealth opposite it

This palace faces 財帛 Wealth, and the pairing is one of the most pointed things the tradition does.

It will not let money be read alone. Every reading of the wealth palace has to be taken against the question of whether the person is content, and a chart where those two answers diverge is saying something worth hearing. A busy wealth palace opposite a thin fortune palace is a recognisable pattern, and it is not a comfortable one.

Its trine palaces, Travel and Spouse, complete the 三方四正 influence set.

The star supplies the tendency, the palace supplies the subject

The palace names the subject, the star names the tendency and brightness sets the strength.

A settled star here reads as someone who knows what restores them; a restless one reads as someone who has to keep looking. Neither is a verdict, and the second is not a worse life, though it is usually a busier one.

When it is empty

An empty 福德 palace does not mean discontent. 空宮 means no major star landed there, which is ordinary.

It is read through Wealth opposite, borrowing those stars, and that borrowed reading is indirect and weaker than a direct one. Saying so is part of reading honestly.

What sits in yours depends on your birth hour, and the free chart places all twelve.

Common questions

Notes
Is the fortune palace about luck?

No, and the English name is misleading enough that it is worth correcting up front. 福德 reads contentment and what restores a person. It is closer to quality of inner life than to fortune in the sense of good luck, and reading it as a luck meter is the most common error made with this palace.

Why is it read against the wealth palace?

Because 福德 and 財帛 sit directly opposite, and the tradition insists on reading them together. It is a deliberate check: it refuses to let a chart's money palace be read without asking whether the person is actually content, which is a different question and often has a different answer.

What does a strong fortune palace look like in practice?

Usually a clear sense of what restores you and reliable access to it. That is a more ordinary finding than the name suggests, and a more useful one, because it is the sort of thing a person can act on.

What reaches Fortune from across the chart

Computed · 三方四正

A palace is never read alone. The one directly opposite informs it, and two more reach it from across the square at a hundred and twenty degrees. Together those four cells are its influence set, and a star transformed in any of them is felt here.
