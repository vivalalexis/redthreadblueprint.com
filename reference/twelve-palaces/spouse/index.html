Spouse 夫妻

fū qī

How you relate closely, read as a style rather than as a person. The palace of partnership, and one of the two the tradition is most careful with.

What the Spouse palace covers

夫妻 is the palace of close partnership, and what it actually reads is style. How you relate when you are close in: what you reach for, how you handle being committed to someone, and the shape the friction usually takes.

That distinction is doing real work. The tradition is old enough that plenty of popular writing has turned this palace into a prediction machine about a specific future person. It is not one, and this practice does not read it that way.

Read it with Career opposite it

夫妻 faces 官祿 Career across the square, and the pairing is more pointed than it looks.

Both palaces are about committing yourself to something outside you and then living with the terms. A chart that is crowded in one and quiet in the other is saying something about where the commitment goes, and classical practice reads the pair rather than either half alone.

Its trine palaces, Fortune and Travel, complete the 三方四正 influence set.

The star supplies the tendency, the palace supplies the subject

The palace fixes the subject. The stars in it decide the tendency, and a strong star here is not a promise of an easy relationship any more than a difficult one is a warning.

What the reading actually produces is a description of your own pattern in close relationships, which is information you can use, rather than a forecast about somebody you have not met.

When it is empty

An empty 夫妻 palace is common, and it does not mean an absence of relationships. 空宮 simply means no major star landed there.

The rule is to read it through Career opposite, borrowing those stars. That reading is indirect and weaker, and an honest reading flags it rather than presenting a borrowed answer at full strength.

What sits in your own depends on your birth hour, and the free chart draws all twelve.

Common questions

Notes
Will this palace describe my future partner?

No, and a page that claims otherwise is selling something the method does not support. 夫妻 reads the style of how you relate closely: what you tend to look for, how you behave when you are in it, and where the friction usually sits. It is a description of you in relationship, not a portrait of somebody who has not arrived yet.

Why does the spouse palace face the career palace?

Because 夫妻 and 官祿 sit directly opposite on the square, and classical practice reads every palace with the one facing it. Partnership and work are treated as a pair: both are places where you commit to something outside yourself, and how you do one tends to inform the other.

Does this palace work the same for any relationship?

It reads close partnership, and this practice keeps that neutral. The subject is the bond, not a role inside it, and nothing in the reading depends on who the partner is.

What reaches Spouse from across the chart

Computed · 三方四正

A palace is never read alone. The one directly opposite informs it, and two more reach it from across the square at a hundred and twenty degrees. Together those four cells are its influence set, and a star transformed in any of them is felt here.
