The eight Kua numbers

Eight Mansions gives every person one number, derived from the birth year and the calculation basis. That number decides which four compass directions work for you and which four do not, and it does not change with the year.

西四命 (West Life group)

Favourable: North-east, North-west, South-west, West

Kua 2 seated South-west Kua 6 seated North-west Kua 7 seated West Kua 8 seated North-east

Both groups hold the same eight designations. Neither has better ground, only different ground.

Why there is no Kua 5

There are nine numbers in the arithmetic and only eight trigram seats on the compass, so one number has nowhere to sit. Five redirects: to 2 on the male calculation basis and to 8 on the female one. Both are west group numbers.

If you have worked out a 5 elsewhere, that is why your number appears to change. A reading keeps the original value on the sheet so you can see what happened rather than wonder.

The year boundary catches people out

The Kua number is derived from the birth year taken at the Li Chun solar boundary in early February, not at 1 January. A birthday in January or the first days of February belongs to the previous year for this purpose.

That single detail is the most common reason a number comes out wrong, and it is why a calculator that asks only for a year without asking anything else can quietly hand you the wrong four directions.
