Why a free tool and a paid reading agree

These tools do not approximate the readings. They import the same functions: the same solar correction, the same calendar tables, the same day-boundary convention. A test run on every build compares the calculator against the readings engine across thousands of births and fails the build if a single one disagrees.

That matters because the alternative is worse than useless. A free tool that hands you one day master and a paid document that hands you another is not a small inconsistency; it is a reason to distrust both.

What these will not do

They draw the chart. They do not interpret it, and they deliberately stop short of the luck pillars 大運, the ten-year cycles a chart runs through. Shown bare, with nothing around them, those are the part of a chart most likely to be read as fate, and reading them properly is the largest single job a reading does.

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The computation runs in your own browser, there is no account step, and no birth data reaches a server, including ours.
