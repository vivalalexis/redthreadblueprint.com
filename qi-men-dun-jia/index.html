Qi Men Dun Jia 奇門遁甲

奇門遁甲 qi men dun jia: the mysterious gates, a plate cast for a moment.

Qi Men Dun Jia does not start from your birth. It casts a plate for the moment a question is asked, which makes it the one system here aimed at a decision rather than a person.

What it is

Where the other systems here read a birth moment, Qi Men Dun Jia reads the moment of asking. A plate is cast for that hour and divided into nine palaces, each carrying a gate, a star and a spirit, and the reading works from where the question and the asker fall on it.

That difference decides what it is for. A birth chart describes tendencies that hold for years. A plate describes the shape of one situation, which is why this reading asks for a question and not just a date.

Your birth chart still matters

The plate is cast for the question, but it is read against your own four pillars, so the reading needs both. That cross-reference is what separates a general plate reading from one addressed to you.
